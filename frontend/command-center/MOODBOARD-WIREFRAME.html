04Animation inventory — the fix for "static"

NPC breathing
All states, continuous subtle chest/shoulder rise
A2
Random blinking
2–6s intervals, per-NPC offset
A3
Typing hands
Working state: arm oscillation at desk
A4
Walk cycle
1–2 NPCs patrol the central aisle on paths
A5
Monitor glow flicker
Per-screen intensity oscillation — office feels powered
A6
Meeting room life
4–5 NPCs around table, gesturing loop
A7
Camera idle drift
Very slow orbit when user not interacting
A8
Plant sway
Subtle rotation on planter foliage
A9
Coffee steam
Tiny rising particles on 2–3 desks
A10
State transitions
WS agent_state → NPC state, 400ms GSAP ease

05Execution pipeline

Step 1
Architecture
Floor, walls, glass cabins, windows+skyline, black ceiling, track lights
Step 2
Furniture
5 bench clusters, chairs, dual monitors, planters, lounge set, props
Step 3
NPCs
25–30 real-proportion avatars, seated/walking/meeting, screen glow, states
Step 4
Lighting + Motion
Blue-white task light, amber baseboards, flicker, camera drift, all 10 anims
Step 5
Integrate + Verify
Orchestrator, WS wiring, dashboard shell UI, browser verification
Steps 1–4 run as parallel delegated subagents; step 5 merges, wires the WebSocket to real agent states, rebuilds the dashboard shell around the scene, and verifies in the live browser at :3000. Brand rule holds: green #56883E only, never neon #2ecc71.
